What's new — this file becomes the text of each release (people see it in the "Update available" box).

How to use it: before raising the version, add a section at the TOP that starts with
"## " and the new version number, then a few short lines. Keep each section under about 600 characters.
If a version has no section here, the release uses your commit messages instead.

## 2.0.2
What's new in 2.0.2

- Progress bars: "Export Saved Data" (⚙ Settings) and the Wishlist's "Export CSV" / "📤 Export to share" now show how far along they are. Exporting your whole library is also faster.
- The sidebar width is saved while you drag it and can't be shrunk back by another screen — it stays the size you made it, also after visiting a Yupoo page.

## 2.0.1
What's new in 2.0.1

- The sidebar keeps the width you dragged it to on every screen, also right after the app opens (no more snapping back). Light/dark mode no longer flashes.
- ⚙ Settings → Your saved data: "Export Saved Data" now saves your whole library and photos as a .zip, "Backup" saves a spreadsheet (CSV), and "Restore" is now called "Import".
- Fix: exported files are dated with your own local date.

## 2.0.0
What's new in 2.0.0

- Much faster: the Library opens in about 1.6 s instead of 8, and filters, search and paging respond quicker.
- New Additions: checks your stores every 2 hours (or ⟳ Check now) and fills in stores you only partly saved.
- Locked stores: enter their password right in the app and see straight away if it's correct.
- Password-locked albums are saved too; better categories (NBA jerseys, watches, glasses, belts…).
- Store search box, Wishlist (was Catalog), Export/Backup/Restore moved to ⚙ Settings.
- Photos take about half the space (existing ones are shrunk once, in the background).

## 1.0.4
What's new in 1.0.4

- ✨ New Additions: each time you open the app, it checks your stores for items they've added since last time and shows them on their own page (top of the Library sidebar), marked NEW.
- Opening a store now goes to its all-categories page, and "Save whole store" reads that page — so it saves every item, not just part of the store.

## 1.0.3
What's new in 1.0.3

- ⚙ Settings page: choose the language, change where cover photos are saved (your photos are moved for you), and see where your library and exports are kept.
- 中文: the whole app can now be switched to Chinese.
- 🩺 Check-up in Settings: finds and explains anything that isn't working.
- Pasting a link for a store you already have now warns you straight away.
- Fix: updating can no longer get stuck offering the same version again.
